# Depth growth of a graph net, 2026-10-23

The depth half of CARD-NET-EXPAND, built for DEEPEN-1. A trained graph net of L GINE layers (any graph arch kind)
becomes the same arch kind at L′ > L layers. At the moment of growth its outputs equal the parent's. Like the widening,
it is a warm-start source, not a new kind: the deeper net is written as a stamped checkpoint, and an arm declares it as
`identity.warm_start` like any other.

## The mechanism (`src/mantis/model/deepen.py`)

The new layers are appended after the parent's L layers. Each starts as an identity. Their JK slots are read by zero
columns.

| tensor | rule |
|---|---|
| `input_proj`, `edge_proj`, `final_norm`; layers 0…L−1 (`convs.i.*`, `norms.i.*`) | carried |
| a new layer's last message linear (`convs.j.nn.2` weight and bias, j ≥ L) | zero |
| a new layer's other tensors (`lin`, `nn.0`, `norms.j`, `eps`) | the fresh draw, seeded |
| the JK readers: both policy heads' and the future head's `mlp.0` weight, the value head's `fc1` weight | the parent's columns, then zero columns for each new slot, per pooled group (V2's `fc1` reads mean ⊕ max, two groups) |
| the heads' biases and second layers | carried |

A tensor that no rule names is refused, so a new module cannot deepen silently. A parent that is not the declared
depth is refused too, and so is a depth that adds no layer.

## Why the function holds

- Layer i computes x_{i+1} = ReLU(conv_i(LN_i(x_i)) + x_i), and conv_i ends in `nn.2`. In a new layer `nn.2` is zero,
  so the conv adds exactly 0 and the layer returns ReLU(x_L).
- x_L is a ReLU's output (L ≥ 1), so ReLU(x_L) = x_L. Every new layer passes x through bit for bit, and every new JK slot
  holds `final_norm(x_L)`, which is the parent's last slot.
- Each head reads the parent's slots through the parent's columns and the new slots through zeros. Each head
  pre-activation therefore equals the parent's, up to the order of a longer sum.

## The known-bad

`known_bad` keeps the fresh draws in the new layers' `nn.2` and in the new head columns. That is the naive deepening
(fresh layers spliced in). Its step-0 outputs move: max |Δ| 0.54–0.98 in fp64 on the seeded 16×2 parents grown to 4 layers.

Fresh layers behind zero columns alone would not move the outputs. They would still fail the trunk half of the pin
below, because a new slot no longer equals the last one.

## Training off the identity

At step 0 nothing reads a new slot, so the new layers get exactly zero gradient, and only the zero columns move.
From step 2 the new layers' last linears move as well. The conformance row pins this order. Under AdamW the first
step moves every zero column by about the learning rate, in the sign of its gradient. This coherent push is the
known cost of a zero init. A continuation read sees through it.

## Pins

- `tests/model/conformance/test_deepening_preserves_outputs.py`:
  - **The fp64 pin, at 1e-4.** For every graph arch kind, a seeded parent (weights, gains and eps off their init)
    grown by one and by two layers holds the pin on the golden r8 positions
    (`tests/fixtures/graph_build/positions_r8.txt`). It covers every head through `forward_batch_heads` (batched and
    single-graph) and the deploy twin `forward_single`. It also covers the trunk: the parent's JK slots are kept, and
    each new slot equals the last.
  - **Both candidate depths of the shape of record.** 4×256 → 5×256 and 6×256 hold the same pin.
  - **The planted break.** The known-bad reds the pin.
  - **The construction.** The carried tensors equal the parent's, the zero rows and columns are zero, and the seed
    fixes the fresh draws.
  - **Refusals.** Each refusal fires.
- The arch's conformance rows also cover depth:
  - the memory envelope's terms move with the declared depth;
  - the floor probe's net follows it;
  - a minted config's declared depth reaches the selected net, which serves;
  - every shipped shape, deepened by two, is reached by a config that declares the deeper depth, and its state
    loads strictly.
- **The stamp** (`tools/distill.py deepen`). The deeper checkpoint's lineage is the parent's plus `+deepen(L->L′)`, or
  `+deepen-known-bad(L->L′)` for the known-bad. Its deploy net is checked to be the deeper net, with EMA off.
