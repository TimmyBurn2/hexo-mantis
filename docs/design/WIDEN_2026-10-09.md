# Width doubling of a graph net (Net2Net), 2026-10-09

The width half of CARD-NET-EXPAND, built for DISTILL-2 (R390(b)): a trained `GnnArch` / `GnnArchV2` /
`GnnArchV2SoftPolicy` net of trunk width H becomes the same arch kind at 2H whose outputs equal the parent's at the moment
of widening. It is a warm-start source, not a new kind: the widened net is written as a stamped checkpoint and an arm
declares it as `identity.warm_start` like any other. Depth waits.

## The mechanism (`src/mantis/model/widen.py`)

Every trunk unit is copied once, so a trunk vector `x` (H) becomes `[x, x]` (2H), and the JK concat of L layers becomes
`[x₁, x₁, …, x_L, x_L]`.

| tensor | rule |
|---|---|
| `input_proj`, `edge_proj` weight and bias; each conv's `lin`, `nn.0`, `nn.2` bias | output rows copied |
| each conv's `lin`, `nn.0`, `nn.2` weight | output rows copied; input columns split |
| `norms.*`, `final_norm` gain and bias | copied |
| both policy heads' `mlp.0` weight, the value head's `fc1` weight | input columns split (each JK block, and the mean and max halves of V2's readout) |
| GINE `eps`, the heads' biases and second layers | unchanged |

A split column: a weight w that reads unit u becomes w·(½ + ε) on u's first copy and w·(½ − ε) on its second.

## Why the function holds

- A Linear reading `[x, x]` through split columns computes Σ w(½+ε)x + w(½−ε)x = Σ wx; with copied rows it emits
  `[y, y]`.
- LayerNorm over `[x, x]`: the mean and the (population) variance over 2H equal those over H, so it emits
  `[LN(x), LN(x)]` with copied gain and bias.
- The GINE message relu(x_src + e), its sum, the degree divisor, `(1 + eps)·x`, the residual and the ReLUs act per unit.
- The stone-masked mean and the max over real nodes act per unit, so V2's pooled vector is `[mean ⊕ max]` with every
  block doubled, which `fc1`'s split columns read back to the parent's value.

Only a uniform replication keeps LayerNorm's statistics, which is why the mechanism doubles and nothing else.

## The split noise

With ε = 0 the two copies of a unit receive identical gradients forever and the wide net stays a narrow one. ε is drawn
uniform in ±0.1 per original weight element (seeded; the same ε on copied output rows), so the pair still sums to w —
the outputs do not move — while the copies' outgoing weights differ and their gradients differ from the first step.
|ε| < ½ keeps both shares positive, so no copy's contribution changes sign.

## Pins

- `tests/model/conformance/test_widening_preserves_outputs.py`: for every graph arch kind, a seeded parent (weights,
  gains and eps off their init) and its doubling agree within 1e-4 on the graph builder's golden r8 positions
  (`tests/fixtures/graph_build/positions_r8.txt`), through `forward_batch_heads` (batched and single-graph) and the deploy
  twin `forward_single`; the 4×128 → 4×256 shape of record likewise; the copies read alike, their readers sum to the
  parent's weight, and only a nonzero split lets them train apart; a tensor without a rule is refused.
- The stamp: the widened checkpoint's lineage is the parent's plus `widened-from-<parent stem>`
  (`tools/distill widen`).
