## C1 — facts at HEAD (A14)

1. **The 64-sim quick search draws root Gumbel noise.** `run_mcts_search` (`crates/mantis-selfplay/src/runner/
   search_drive.rs`) builds `MctxRootState::new` on every Gumbel search, whatever `move_is_full_search` drew, and
   `MctxRootState::new` draws one Gumbel(0,1) per root child with no scale term. From the absolute ply
   `gumbel_explore_moves` (10) on, `select_move` plays `best_action`, whose score carries that noise, so the quick arm's
   played move is noised too; before it the move is sampled from the visit policy. The quick arm's row trains value
   only (`train.fast_policy_weight: 0.0`).
2. **What the ring carries** (`GraphRecord` / `HexgBuffer`): stones, the m explicit visit entries and the tail mass α,
   the side to move, `moves_remaining`, `ply_index`, `is_full_search`, the outcome z, `value_valid`, `game_length`,
   `game_id`, the sampling weight. **No root value**: the search's root value is recorded only in the game record's
   `search_stats`, one game in `selfplay.search_stats_every` (8). **No next-ply target field**. **Game order: yes**, by
   `game_id` and `ply_index`, so a next-ply target is the next row's, present only when that row is in the ring and
   full-arm. On the frozen 45k ring: 100 000 rows, 1 239 games, 0 untagged, no ply gaps (1 238 games start at ply 0),
   25 309 full-arm rows; 25 002 rows have a full-arm successor.
3. **`value_valid` against the live masking: the doc is right, the research's reading was not.** `finalize_graph_outcome`
   returns `(ply_cap_value, 0)` for `terminal_reason == 2` and both trainer value-loss call sites pass
   `value_mask=inputs.value_valid` (`dist65.binned_value_loss` drops masked rows from numerator and denominator), so a
   ply-capped row's stored −0.5 reaches no loss. An organic draw (`terminal_reason 3`) trains at `train.draw_reward`
   (−0.5 in run10). `finalize.rs`'s module doc called `value_valid` the "DRAW-MASK" and `finalize_graph_outcome`'s doc
   the "draw-mask": both now say ply-cap mask.
4. **`new_game`'s comment was stale**: every driver (`play_one_move`, `DeployHeadPlayer.select_move`, the analyzer,
   `ring_audit`) calls it before each search, so the TT lives one search, not one game; repaired in place.
5. The in-search TT key is the stone set with colours. The stone count fixes the side to move and the stones left, so
   the key is the position.

## C2 — the server's `launch` split (A1)

Rule (before the reading): the named parts sum to `launch` within 10 % at every B, else that cell is unsplit; the timers
cost < 5 % of leaves/s; a least-squares line launch = a + b·B over B ∈ {32, 64, 128}, a ≥ 3 ms a per-pop fixed cost.
Instrument: the tree's `tools/bench_server.py` cells (the real batcher and `InferenceServer`, 32 workers × 8 leaves,
`compile_trunk` on, run8@45k, positions from run8's events) with timers on the functions `_launch_pop` looks up at call
time. Host: the desktop RTX 3070 and 16 cores, so every ms here is the desktop's; the box's 12.3 ms/pop is not the
comparand. Tree `986a9661`.

| B | leaves/s (median, IQR) | launch | copies (H2D) | collate less copies | trunk launch | heads + readout | bookkeeping | softmax + D2H | residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 32 | 2 092 [2 035, 2 120] | 12.76 | 4.24 | 1.96 | 3.56 | 1.59 | 0.64 | 0.38 | 0.39 (3.1 %) |
| 64 | 2 543 [2 507, 2 576] | 19.87 | 7.60 | 3.80 | 3.99 | 2.53 | 0.68 | 0.54 | 0.74 (3.7 %) |
| 128 (B 124.7) | 2 205 [2 028, 2 212] | 48.57 | 15.35 | 10.13 | 6.91 | 3.96 | 1.15 | 0.89 | 10.19 (21 %): UNSPLIT |

(ms per pop; `gpu_wait` 6.55 / 14.28 / 22.55 ms; "copies" is the collate's host-side pinned staging and enqueue, not
the DMA itself.) The untimed B 64 cell read 2 544 leaves/s: timed / untimed 0.9996 by median, 0.990 by mean.

- **The H2D copies are the largest part** (33–38 % of `launch`, 0.132 / 0.119 ms a leaf at B 32 / 64), then the
  compiled trunk's launch, which is **nearly fixed per forward** (3.6 → 4.0 ms from B 32 to 64; what fills it, guards or
  kernel launches, was not measured), then the collate's own work (≈ 0.06 ms a leaf).
- **The B-curve by the rule's line: a = −2.2 ms, b = 0.40 ms a leaf — below the 3 ms line, and uninformative.** At
  run10's fused-graph caps (`max_fused_edges` 1 373 143) a full B 128 pop (≈ 1.96 M edges) is planned as two or more
  forwards, so the three points mix one-forward and two-forward pops (B 128's trunk launch, 6.91 ms, is about twice B 64's).
  Descriptively, from B 32 to 64 alone the line reads a = 5.7 ms, the trunk's ≈ 3.1–3.5 ms of it per forward. The
  rule's per-part line was not read, since B 128 is unsplit. The B 128 residual (10.2 ms) is untimed work; the likely
  home, a guess, is `slice_graph_wire`, which converts the whole `edge_index` per part, beside the smaller untimed items
  (the plan's bound checks, the `legal_offsets` copy, casts, the lock and autocast entries, the CUDA event).
- At these caps B 64 is the best cell on this host and B 128 is 13 % slower. The split's first lever is cheaper copies
  (the levers' C1: one pinned buffer); a per-forward trunk cost of ≈ 3.5 ms is what larger caps or CUDA graphs would
  amortise.
- Owed by the rule and not captured (this leg's error): the per-window spread of `launch` beside the line. The bench
  keeps per-window leaves/s only; those windows spread 1 901–2 133 (B 32), 1 717–2 618 (B 64) and 1 840–2 215 (B 128),
  one slow window in every cell, the untimed control's too. The cell re-runs in about 5 minutes.

## C3 — counters and tails (A7, A8)

### The counter

`MCTSTree.tt_hits` counts, per select call, the descents `select_leaves` backs up with the table's value: a
transposition, never served. The runner sums it into `tt_hits_total` (snapshot, `SelfPlayRunner.tt_hits_total`,
`RunnerStats`, the `search_levers` row of `iteration_complete`); `MCTSTree.last_tt_hits` is bridged. It reads 0 under
Gumbel, whose forced select has no table path. It landed at `7f18b478` (the tree RULES_C3 names) and its review moved
the definition at `986a9661`: a terminal revisit on the table path backs up its own value and is no longer counted.
Every TT-hit reading below is on `986a9661`'s definition. Pinned by `crates/mantis-search/tests/tt_hit_count.rs` (on
every PUCT select call the root rises by exactly the hits plus the terminal revisits; two planted breaks red it),
`crates/mantis-selfplay/tests/tt_hits_counted.rs` (the runner row) and the bridge round-trip test. It is a count only:
this tree's table hits are uncharged against the budget, and TACTICS-SELFPLAY's R378(c) fix makes them charged
descents, so a TT-hit share read here and one read after that fix are different quantities.

### (d) In-search TT hits at deploy

Rule (before the reading): the pooled TT-hit share of descents (hits / (served + inline + hits)) at the plain deploy
head (PUCT 256, c_puct 1.5, fpu 0.25, tactics null) over the same 2 000 roots and their successors; > 10 % → graph

the quantity MCGS would merge. The rule's control, `tt_hits_counted.rs` green on this tree with its planted break

- **Turn starts (the primary set): 0.286 [0.279, 0.294] of descents** (SE 0.39 pp; 99 hits per search beside 248
  served leaves); mid-turn 0.177 [0.168, 0.186] (SE 0.45 pp); both sets pooled 0.236.
- **Verdict: 28.6 % > 10 %: MCGS earns a deploy A/B.**
- Starvation, read beside it: 122 of 2 000 turn-start searches (6.1 %) and 146 mid-turn (7.3 %) ended short, 15 929 and
