## 2. Boxes and schedule

- Two matched boxes (4080S): box A = `vast`, box B = the alias the operator names. Arms run in pairs on the two boxes
  at once: (1, 2), then (3, 4); all four at once if four matched boxes are rented. Arm 1 and arm 3 on box A.
- Each box: one preflight (`tools/ci_gates/preflight_mint.py --config <arm>`, ≤ 0.5 box-h) on the arm's tree and
  config before its first arm.
- Each arm runs 8 h wall-clock from its launch, then ONE SIGTERM: the run saves (the final save) and exits. A signal
  stop skips the terminal eval, and the final save carries its ring, so the picked arm resumes from it.
- The box is otherwise IDLE while an arm runs: no cell, no bench, no other session's job.

## 3. The monitor, at every save

`tools/run_monitor.py follow` beside each arm on its box, `--halt` armed, written to the arm's records
(`<box>:/workspace/records/<run id>/`, mirrored to `mantis-records/run11-pre/<run id>/`):

```
python tools/run_monitor.py follow --run-dir /workspace/runs/<run id> --run-id <run id> \
  --out /workspace/records/<run id> --gen-ring <GEN held-out ring> \
  --gen-sha256 5738d01f506191f9a24754e716a75d5e2bd410ce4c202499708e7f0f75822f93 \
  --exams <exam_positions.jsonl> --exams-sha256 ec069e7f8b1f6c633d05559500f59d3f7a1a4af998527799f70536198c6baaa4 \
  --bands docs/design/RUN11_PREREG_2026-10-04.md --floors T4_V=0.154,DEF_V_att=0.100 \
  --parent checkpoints/run8/run8_00045000_3bdedf76.ckpt --batches 320 --device cuda --threads 4 --halt
```

Per save s it records:
- **The GEN anchor:** the instrument of record (`tools/value_instrument` read, 320 batches, seed 20260929, fold seed
  20261002) on GEN's held-out ring (1 800 games of run8@45k under arm A's regime, REG-1): calibration-free CE, T,
  AUC, by ply band, and policy CE.
- **The paired lagged read (lag one save):** when save s+1 lands, the games produced after save s (the rows of
  ring(s+1) above ring(s)'s last game id) are read by net s and by its lagged net (net s−1; the parent for the first
  save) on the same draws. L_s = cf_CE(net s) − cf_CE(net s−1), with its game-bootstrap CI; net s's read carries its
  ring(s) as the train side, so the in-distribution gap is recorded with it.
- **The calibrated exams:** T4 V (261 positions) and DEF V_att (231), each net's raw value at the position calibrated
  `tanh(atanh V / T)` at that save's GEN temperature. The reader reproduces the parent's 0B values bit for bit
  (T4 V 0.42564 raw / 0.28592 calibrated, DEF V_att 0.36960 / 0.20999;
  `mantis-records/run11-pre/witness/exams_known_good.py`).
- **The ring bands** (§4) on ring(s), with the newest events segment.
- **The rates:** games/h, positions/h, steps/h over the save interval, and the same over the monitor's idle windows;
  realised steps per game = steps/h ÷ games/h.

**Halting rows (the arm HALTS at once, ONE SIGTERM, and is reported):** calibrated T4 V < 0.154 or DEF V_att < 0.100
at any save; a ring band outside at any save; the run's own aborts as minted (draw-rate, ply-cap, actor lag, disk).

## 5. The cells, at each arm's final save

The arm's shipped head v X (`tools/strix_follower.py --once <final ckpt> --unit six30_16 --arm full --tactics-block
full_block.json --games 576`: 288 pairs, ≈ 25 min at the parent's 2.53 s/game), on the IDLE box after the arm stops. The parent anchor (run8@45k, the same
command under the template config) is re-read once per box. A cell is a screen at ±0.17 logit: it decides nothing
but a known-bad — an arm whose cell reads below its box's parent anchor by more than 0.17 logit is not pickable.

## 6. The pick

Among arms whose halting rows never fired and that are not known-bad (§5):
1. Rank by the paired lagged value reading L averaged over the arm's last three computed saves (the most negative
   best: the value head improving fastest on games it never trained on).
2. Ties within the line (0.012 nats) are broken by policy CE on the GEN ring at the final save (lower better), then by
   positions/h (the monitor's idle-window rate over the arm).
3. Arm 1 is picked only if no masked arm beats it by the line (a masked arm's L below arm 1's by ≥ 0.012).
4. Arm 4 is picked over arm 3 only if its value and policy readings are not worse than arm 3's by the line and its
   positions/h is higher by ≥ 15 %. Arm 4's cap rate and draw share are reported against F-02/F-52's attractor
   (stated now: the windowed cap rate leaving run8's 0.16–2.2 % and climbing — shakedown7 went 0.11 → 0.84 in four
   hours — or the median game length rising toward the 256-ply cap).

Report-only beside the pick, never ruling: L per hour (L × saves/h), the GEN anchor's level at the final save, the
calibrated exams' trajectory, the gap.

## 7. The dip read

(reported).

else HALT and the architect rules.

## 10. Halts, power and biases

**HALT:** a halting row (§3); a config armed without the word; an arm started before this file's hash; a pin red;
a priced act beyond the grant (L2 ≤ 40 box-h across two boxes plus preflights).

**Power.** The instrument reads at seed SD ≈ 0.003 on ≥ 1 800 held-out games; two arms resolve ≥ 0.012 nats at
power ≥ 0.8. A save interval holds ≈ 1 250 games at 2.4 steps/game and ≈ 2 500 at 1.2, so one L_s is noisier at 2.4;
the average over three saves is the ruling read. The cells are screens.

**Biases, stated before the data:**
- L is improvement per 3 000 steps, not per hour: an arm at 1.2 steps/game sees twice the games per save and makes
  fewer saves per hour. The per-hour row is reported beside it.
- L is relative to the arm's own previous save; it rewards a head still improving, so an arm that over-fits reads
  L ≥ 0 (the signal REG-1 priced) and an arm that saturated early also reads near 0.
- GEN is the parent's games: the anchor drifts off-distribution as an arm moves.
- The monitor shares its arm's GPU for ≈ 6 min per save, so arms with more saves per hour lose more; the idle-window
  rates remove most of that from positions/h, not from steps.
- The ring bands are re-derived on one frozen-actor ring.
- One seed per arm; the pick is a screen-level read of the step rate, not a strength claim.
