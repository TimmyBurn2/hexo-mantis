## 2. Boxes and schedule

- Two matched boxes (4080S): box A = `vast`, box B = the alias the operator names. Arms run in pairs on the two boxes
  at once: (1, 2), then (3, 4) — arm 1 then arm 3 on box A, arm 2 then arm 4 on box B; all four at once if four
  matched boxes are rented.
- **One preflight per arm config** (`tools/ci_gates/preflight_mint.py --config configs/<arm>.yaml`, ≤ 0.5 box-h each,
  four in all): the launch refuses a config identity no preflight stamped, and the four identities differ. The box's
  first preflight also runs the monitor beside it (`once` on the preflight's save) to measure its card memory.
- Each arm runs 8 h wall-clock from its launch; a timer on the box then sends ONE SIGTERM: the run saves (the final
  save) and exits. A signal stop skips the terminal eval, and the final save carries its ring, so the picked arm
  resumes from it.
- The box is otherwise IDLE while an arm runs: no cell, no bench, no other session's job. Cells start only after the
  arm's process and its monitor have exited.

## 3. The monitor, at every save

`tools/run_monitor.py follow` beside each arm on its box, `--halt` armed, written to the arm's records
(`/workspace/records/<run id>/`, mirrored to `mantis-mirror/run11-pre/<run id>/` by a desktop puller every 10 min):

```
python tools/run_monitor.py follow --run-dir /workspace/runs/<run id> --run-id <run id> \
  --out /workspace/records/<run id> --gen-ring /workspace/run11-inputs/heldout.bin \
  --gen-sha256 5738d01f506191f9a24754e716a75d5e2bd410ce4c202499708e7f0f75822f93 \
  --exams /workspace/run11-inputs/exam_positions.jsonl \
  --exams-sha256 ec069e7f8b1f6c633d05559500f59d3f7a1a4af998527799f70536198c6baaa4 \
  --bands docs/design/RUN11_PREREG_2026-10-04.md --bands-sha256 <this file's hash, §12> \
  --floors T4_V=0.154,DEF_V_att=0.100 --line 0.012 --parent checkpoints/run8/run8_00045000_3bdedf76.ckpt \
  --batches 320 --device cuda --gpu-mem-fraction <measured at the first preflight> --threads 4 --halt
```

Per save s it records:
- **The GEN anchor:** the instrument of record (`tools/value_instrument` read, 320 batches, seed 20260929, fold seed
  20261002) on GEN's held-out ring (1 800 games of run8@45k under arm A's regime, REG-1): calibration-free CE, T,
  AUC, by ply band, and policy CE.
- **The paired lagged read (lag one save):** when save s+1 lands, the games produced after save s (the rows of
  ring(s+1) whose game id is above ring(s)'s last; a game straddling the save is excluded) are read by net s and by
  its lagged net (net s−1; the parent for the first save) on the same draws. L_s = cf_CE(net s) − cf_CE(net s−1),
  with its game-bootstrap CI and its game count; net s's read carries ring(s) as the train side, so the
  in-distribution gap is recorded with it.
- **The calibrated exams:** T4 V (261 positions) and DEF V_att (231), each net's raw value at the position calibrated
  `tanh(atanh V / T)` at that save's GEN temperature (320 batches, as the floors were derived). The reader reproduces
  the parent's 0B values bit for bit (T4 V 0.42564 raw / 0.28592 calibrated, DEF V_att 0.36960 / 0.20999;
  `mantis-records/run11-pre/witness/exams_known_good.py`).
- **The ring bands** (§4) on ring(s), with the newest events segment.
- **The rates:** games/h, positions/h, steps/h over the save interval, and the same over the monitor's idle windows;
  realised steps per game = steps/h ÷ games/h; the quick arm's round width
  (`iteration_complete.search_levers.gumbel_quick_round_leaves / gumbel_quick_rounds`, arm 4's m in-run).

**Halting rows (the arm HALTS at once, ONE SIGTERM, and is reported):** calibrated T4 V < 0.154 or DEF V_att < 0.100
at any save; a ring band outside at any save; the run's own aborts as minted (draw-rate, ply-cap, actor lag, disk).
A save whose ring was pruned before it was read is NOT MEASURED, never a miss. A halting row at the final save is
recorded and not signalled (the run is already stopping). The monitor persists its state, so a restart reads no save
twice and never re-sends a halt; a failed read is recorded and the follow continues; `monitor_alive.json` is its
liveness. Its cost is ≈ 10–15 min per save (two GEN-sized reads, one ring audit ≈ 6 min of one core, the exams).

## 5. The cells, at each arm's final save

The arm's shipped head v X (`tools/strix_follower.py --once <final ckpt> --unit six30_16 --arm full --tactics-block
full_block.json --games 576`: 288 pairs, ≈ 25 min at the parent's 2.53 s/game), on the IDLE box after the arm and its
monitor stop. The parent anchor (run8@45k, the same command under the box's first arm config) is re-read once per box.
A cell is a screen at ±0.17 logit and decides nothing but a known-bad: **(stated as a deviation for the operator)**
the pick (§6) does not read the cells; if the PICKED arm's cell reads below its box's parent anchor by more than
0.17 logit, the continuation does not start and the architect rules. At zero true difference that happens ≈ 10 % of
the time (two 288-pair cells, SE of their difference ≈ 0.13 logit).

## 6. The pick

The value reading is L̄, an arm's paired lagged reading averaged over its last three computed saves, each weighted by
its unseen games (the last interval is partial). The line is 0.012 nats.
1. Pickable arms: no halting row ever fired. None pickable: HALT, the architect rules.
2. The best is the most negative L̄ (the value head improving fastest on games it never trained on). Every pickable
   arm within the line of the best is tied with it.
3. Among the tied, the lowest policy CE on the GEN ring at the final save wins by ≥ 0.012 nats (stated: the packet
   names no policy line, so the value line serves); still tied, the highest positions/h (§10's box normalisation).
4. Arm 1 is picked only if no masked arm's L̄ is below arm 1's by the line.
5. Arm 4 is picked over arm 3 only if arm 4's L̄ and GEN policy CE are each not worse than arm 3's by the line and its
   box-normalised positions/h is higher by ≥ 15 %; if arm 3 halted, arm 4 is ranked by 1–3 alone. Arm 4's cap rate
   and draw share are reported against F-02/F-52's attractor (stated now: the windowed cap rate leaving run8's
   0.16–2.2 % and climbing — shakedown7 went 0.11 → 0.84 in four hours — or the median game length rising toward the
   256-ply cap).

Report-only beside the pick, never ruling: L̄ per hour (L × saves/h), the GEN anchor's level at the final save, the
calibrated exams' trajectory, the gap.

## 8. The continuation

The picked arm resumes from its last save with its ring as run11; its run id continues (its stamps are immutable),
in the same run directory, with its monitor resumed from its own state. From then: the monitor at every save; every
fourth save (12 000 steps) a shipped-head cell v the parent anchor on X. GOING-FORWARD: by 36 000 steps the mean of
the last four cells available (the arm's final cell included; fewer than four, those available, stated) is above the
parent anchor by the cell line, 0.17 logit (cells are read in logit; 0.012 nats is the value line), else HALT and
the architect rules.

## 10. Halts, power and biases

**HALT:** a halting row (§3); a config armed without the word; an arm started before this file's hash; a pin red;
a priced act beyond the grant (L2 ≤ 40 box-h across two boxes, plus the four preflights ≈ 2 box-h).

**Power.** The instrument's level reads have a seed SD ≈ 0.003 on ≥ 1 800 held-out games, so two arms' LEVELS
resolve ≥ 0.012 nats at power ≥ 0.8. The pick ranks L̄, a difference of two nets on the same draws: each L carries
its game-bootstrap CI, but its spread between seeds is unmeasured and each arm has one seed. The pick is therefore a
selection, not a detection: LAW-19's power bar is not claimed for it. A save interval holds ≈ 1 250 games at 2.4
steps/game and ≈ 2 500 at 1.2; the weighted three-save average is the read.

**Biases, stated before the data:**
- L is improvement per 3 000 steps, not per hour: an arm at 1.2 steps/game sees twice the games per save and makes
  fewer saves per hour. The per-hour row is reported beside it.
- L is relative to the arm's own previous save: an arm that over-fits reads L ≥ 0 (the signal REG-1 priced), and an
  arm that saturated early also reads near 0.
- Arm 4 against arm 3 compares two boxes. Arms 1 and 2 share their self-play, so their positions/h ratio is the box
  ratio; arm 4's positions/h is divided by it before the 15 % test.
- At matched wall-clock the arms sit at different points of the cosine LR (T_max 108 000): arms 1/2 end near 30k
  steps, arms 3/4 near 15k.
- An arm crossing `train.eval_interval` (36 000) runs an in-run gate round on its box; it is reported, and its window
  is out of the idle-window rates.
- The draw-rate abort (min step 25 000) cannot fire for arms 3/4 inside 8 h.
- GEN is the parent's games: the anchor drifts off-distribution as an arm moves.
- The monitor shares its arm's GPU for 10–15 min per save, so arms with more saves per hour lose more steps; the
  idle-window rates remove most of it from positions/h.
- The ring bands are derived on arm-A-family rings without the fill ramp; the ramp's early rings draw each row less.
- The exam floors' temperature is the 320-batch GEN read's; another batch count reads another T.
- One seed per arm.
